Cookie Policy

Version dated 20 November 2026

Account cookies

EuroLoot uses an HTTP-only cookie to keep you signed in. Sessions last up to 30 minutes. Signing out invalidates the session. A separate cookie binds external sign-in to the browser that started it and expires after ten minutes.

Device storage

The external discovery wishlist and saved picks are stored on your device. Clearing this site’s browser data removes those local lists. Your direct-store wishlist is stored with your account.

Optional tracking

EuroLoot does not enable optional advertising or analytics cookies in this build. The four account-policy acknowledgements do not authorise optional tracking. External retailers have their own cookie settings.
