Summary: This policy explains how EuroLoot uses cookies and similar technologies. The technologies actually used by the website must be reflected accurately in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on, or accessed from, your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may also be used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve security, or perform analytics.

2. Cookies Used by EuroLoot

EuroLoot aims to limit the use of cookies and similar technologies to those necessary for operating the website and providing requested functionality.

The website must be reviewed to determine whether it currently uses:

  • Essential session or authentication cookies.
  • Preference or functionality cookies.
  • Analytics or measurement technologies.
  • Advertising or tracking technologies.
  • Third-party embedded content or services.

If non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, EuroLoot will provide the appropriate information and obtain consent where legally required.

3. External Websites

EuroLoot may link to or redirect users to third-party services, including external gaming stores.

Those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices are governed by their own privacy and cookie policies, not this policy.

4. Consent and Cookie Controls

Under applicable Irish and European privacy law, consent is generally required for non-essential cookies and similar technologies, subject to applicable exceptions for technologies that are strictly necessary for a service requested by the user.

Where consent is required, users should be provided with clear information and an appropriate way to accept, reject, or manage the relevant technologies.

5. Changes to Cookie Usage

EuroLoot may introduce new features or third-party services that change how cookies or similar technologies are used. This policy will be updated when those changes occur.
