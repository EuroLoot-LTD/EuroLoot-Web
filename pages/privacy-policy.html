Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026. Last reviewed: 18 September 2026.

Summary: EuroLoot aims to respect your privacy. We do not sell personal data or use personal data for advertising purposes. The information we collect depends on the features you use and the services required to operate EuroLoot. This policy explains how personal data may be collected, used, stored, and shared.

1. Who We Are

EuroLoot is a European gaming retailer and platform currently in development. EuroLoot is designed to help users discover games, hardware, accessories, collectibles, deals, and other gaming-related products.

EuroLoot may provide purchasing options through its own platform or by directing users to external third-party stores. When a user selects an external purchasing option, the transaction is completed on the relevant third-party platform.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at: euroloot.net@gmail.com.

EuroLoot is not affiliated with Valve Corporation, Steam, Epic Games, GOG, itch.io, Nintendo, Sony, Microsoft, Google, Discord, or any other third-party platform referenced on this website unless explicitly stated otherwise.

2. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on the functionality available on EuroLoot and how you use the service.

  • Contact information: If you contact us by email or another communication method, we may process the information you provide, including your email address and the content of your message.
  • Account information: Where account functionality is available, we may process information needed to create and manage your account, such as your username, email address, account identifier, and authentication-related information.
  • Authentication information: If you sign in using a third-party authentication provider, that provider may share information with us as necessary to authenticate you and provide the requested sign-in service.
  • Technical information: Our hosting providers or security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and requested pages.
  • Website preferences: Depending on the features implemented, EuroLoot may temporarily process information relating to preferences, shopping selections, or other interactions with the website.
  • Purchase information: If EuroLoot introduces direct purchasing, information necessary to process an order may be collected. Payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider rather than stored directly by EuroLoot.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific lawful purpose and appropriate safeguards are in place.

3. How We Use Information

Personal information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Operating, maintaining, and improving EuroLoot.
  • Responding to enquiries and support requests.
  • Managing accounts, where account functionality is available.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the website.
  • Detecting, preventing, and investigating misuse or unlawful activity.
  • Processing orders and providing customer support for direct EuroLoot purchases, where applicable.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services and External Stores

EuroLoot may link to or redirect users to external platforms, including Steam and other gaming stores.

When you visit or use an external platform, that platform may process personal information in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms. EuroLoot does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

EuroLoot currently supports purchasing routes involving Steam and EuroLoot where those options are available. Other platforms may be introduced in the future. We will update our documentation when additional purchasing integrations become operational.

EuroLoot may also use third-party infrastructure providers, hosting providers, APIs, content delivery services, authentication providers, or security services. The actual providers used by the website must be reflected in this policy before publication.

EuroLoot uses third-party providers to operate its services, including Supabase for authentication, database storage, and related backend functionality, and Google for Google sign-in. Additional providers may be used for hosting, analytics, payments, or other features where enabled. Please check the services currently integrated into EuroLoot for the complete and accurate list of providers and how they process your data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Personal information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate EuroLoot, provide requested services, maintain security, process transactions, or comply with legal obligations.

Where you choose to continue to an external store, the relevant store may process information independently of EuroLoot. You should review that provider's privacy policy before completing a transaction.

6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where GDPR applies, EuroLoot will process personal data using an appropriate legal basis, which may include:

  • Contract performance: Where processing is necessary to provide a service or fulfil an agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for purposes such as website security, fraud prevention, or service improvement, provided that your rights and interests are appropriately considered.
  • Consent: Where consent is required and you have freely provided it for a specified purpose.
  • Legal obligation: Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Specific retention periods depend on the type of information and the systems used by EuroLoot. We will review retention periods periodically and delete or anonymise information when it is no longer required, unless a longer retention period is legally necessary.

8. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • Access to personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of personal data in appropriate circumstances.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Objection to certain processing activities.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact: euroloot.net@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie.

9. Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should avoid sending sensitive information through unsecured or unnecessary communication channels.

10. Children's Privacy

EuroLoot is designed for users in the European Union. Where a feature involves the collection of personal information from children or young people, EuroLoot will apply appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. International Transfers

Some service providers used by EuroLoot may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, we will take steps to ensure that any international transfer is carried out in accordance with applicable data protection law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: euroloot.net@gmail.com.
