Privacy Policy

Version dated 20 November 2026

Data used to operate your account

EuroLoot records your account identifier, account country, profile settings and dated acceptance of the four account policies. Your email or chosen identity provider verifies your sign-in. The account service uses this information to authenticate you, protect access and remember your settings.

Retail and support records

The retail service records your basket, wishlist, order history, entitlements, support correspondence and developer submissions. Access is limited by account and staff role. Request only the information needed for support and avoid including payment or identity documents in a ticket.

Providers and location

The hosted configuration uses Supabase for identity and Cloudflare for request delivery and storage. Where test checkout is enabled, Stripe processes the hosted checkout. The regional gate uses a country code from the network connection. Local test accounts and orders are stored on the computer running the local service.

Your account data

Open Account to download your account data or sign out all sessions. Contact EuroLoot through a Privacy ticket or the address below to request access, correction, erasure or other assistance. The discovery wishlist saved in your browser can be cleared through site settings.

Contact EuroLoot at euroloot.net@gmail.com.
