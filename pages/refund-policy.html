Refunds and test orders

Draft for staging and legal review

A local test order does not transfer money. The operator can refund the fixture order and revoke its entitlement. Open a Refund support ticket and include the order reference.

Purchases through Steam or another external retailer are processed by that retailer. Contact the seller shown on your receipt.

Live EuroLoot payments are closed. Statutory rights and the terms for digital supply, cancellation and refunds must be reviewed before sales open.
