EuroLoot / Support

Refund Policy

Version dated 20 November 2026

Find your seller

Check the seller named on your order or receipt. If you followed an external offer, contact that retailer about payments, keys and refunds.

EuroLoot orders

For a direct order, open Support, choose Refund and include your order reference. Do not include card details. Support can review your request and the status of your entitlement.

Test orders

A local test order transfers no money. Refunding it revokes access to its download. The test file saves to your device and an existing local copy cannot be recalled.

Consumer rights

Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable. Commercial EuroLoot payments are currently closed.

Contact EuroLoot at euroloot.net@gmail.com.
