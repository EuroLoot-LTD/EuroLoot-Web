Refund Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026. Last reviewed: 18 September 2026.

This Refund Policy explains how refund enquiries are handled depending on whether a purchase is completed through an external store or directly through EuroLoot.

1. External Store Purchases

When you select an external purchasing option on EuroLoot, you may be redirected to a third-party platform to complete the transaction.

For example, selecting a Steam purchasing option redirects you to Steam, where the purchase is completed under Valve Corporation's applicable terms and refund policy.

For purchases completed through an external store, refund requests should generally be submitted to the store or seller that processed the transaction.

EuroLoot does not control the external store's refund decisions, payment systems, fulfilment arrangements, or customer support processes.

2. Steam Purchases

For purchases completed through Steam, Valve Corporation's applicable refund policy and purchasing terms apply.

You can review Steam's refund information at: store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds.

Refund eligibility depends on the purchase, product, applicable policy, and consumer rights that apply to the transaction.

3. Direct EuroLoot Purchases

If EuroLoot processes a purchase directly, the refund terms applicable to that product and transaction will be provided through the relevant purchasing process.

Refunds, cancellations, delivery issues, defective products, and other consumer remedies will be handled in accordance with applicable Irish and European Union law.

This section applies only to direct EuroLoot transactions that are operational and clearly identified as such.

4. Statutory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict statutory consumer rights.

Depending on the type of product or service, you may have rights relating to cancellation, defective goods, digital content, delivery, or other consumer remedies. The applicable rights depend on the transaction, seller, product, and relevant law.

5. Contact and Support

If you are unsure which platform processed your purchase or need help identifying the appropriate refund process, contact: euroloot.net@gmail.com.

When contacting us, avoid sending unnecessary payment details, passwords, or other sensitive information.

6. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund Policy when EuroLoot introduces new purchasing functionality or when applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
